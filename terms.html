Terms and conditions

Last updated 11 September 2026. Applies to restaurants who subscribe and to anyone who uses a chakh menu at a table.

1. What the service is

chakh provides a web-based menu layer. Diners reach it by scanning a QR code or tapping an NFC card at a restaurant table. It explains dishes, pronounces their names, displays nutrition and allergen information supplied by the restaurant, and passes orders and modifications to the restaurant.

2. Allergens — read this one

chakh displays ingredient and allergen information. It does not determine it. Every declaration comes from the restaurant, which is solely responsible for its accuracy and for keeping it current when a recipe, supplier or preparation method changes.

Kitchens handle many ingredients in shared spaces and cross-contact is possible in any restaurant. A diner with a serious allergy should speak to restaurant staff before ordering. chakh is an aid to that conversation, not a substitute for it, and gives no medical or dietary advice.

Nutrition figures are the restaurant's own estimates and will vary between plates.

3. What the AI does and doesn't do

Dish explanations are generated with AI and then reviewed and approved by the restaurant before any diner sees them. Follow-up answers are generated live and constrained to the approved text and declared ingredients. They may still occasionally be wrong or incomplete. Treat them as a knowledgeable host's description, not as a specification. Anything that matters — allergies, religious restrictions, medical needs — should be confirmed with the restaurant.

4. Restaurant obligations

  • Supply accurate menu, ingredient and allergen information, and update it when it changes.
  • Review and approve dish content before it goes live.
  • Hold the rights to any text, photographs or video you give us to publish.
  • Keep your own account credentials secure.

5. Fees

Plans and prices are on the pricing section of our home page. Subscriptions bill monthly in advance and can be cancelled with 30 days' notice, effective at the end of the current billing month. Setup fees are one-time and non-refundable once the menu has been built. Taxes are extra where applicable. We will give 30 days' written notice before changing your price.

6. Free trial

The first 30 days on Pro are free. If your average bill has not improved over that period, you owe nothing and may walk away. We will agree how it is being measured in writing before the trial starts.

7. Who owns what

You keep everything you give us: your menu, your recipes, your brand, your photographs. We keep the platform, the software and the general dish knowledge base, including explanations of dishes that are common to many kitchens. If you leave, we will export your menu data to you and delete your account data within 60 days on request.

8. Availability

We aim for 99.5% monthly uptime, excluding planned maintenance we've told you about and failures at third parties like hosting or network providers. If a diner cannot load the menu, the printed menu on the table is the fallback — which is why we ask every restaurant to keep one.

9. Acceptable use

Don't use chakh to publish anything unlawful, misleading or infringing, don't try to break into it, scrape it wholesale, or resell it without a written agreement.

11. Ending it

Either side may terminate with 30 days' notice. We may suspend an account immediately for non-payment after written notice, or for a breach of clause 9.
