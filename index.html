The problem

The menu went digital. It never got smarter.

A QR code turned your printed menu into a PDF on a phone. Same words, smaller type. The guest who didn't know what a dish was still doesn't.

They don't ask

Asking a waiter to explain six dishes at a full table feels like an imposition. Most people won't.

They play safe

So the regional dish you're proud of loses to the one item on the menu everybody recognises.

Allergies live in someone's memory

Even top restaurants print "tell your server if you're allergic" and hope it gets passed to the kitchen correctly, mid-rush.

How it works

Three taps, then the menu knows who it's talking to.

1 · Tap the card

QR or NFC. Opens in the browser. Nothing to download, nothing to sign up for.

2 · Tell it once

Veg or not, anything you avoid, which language. Every dish that's a problem gets flagged by name.

3 · Ask anything

What is this. Is it spicy. How do I say it. Answers in English, Kannada or Hindi, in about two seconds.

Every explanation is written once, approved by your kitchen, and then locked. Nothing reaches a guest that a chef hasn't signed off.
