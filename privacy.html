Privacy policy

Last updated 11 September 2026. Written to be read, not survived.

Who we are

chakh is operated by [YOUR REGISTERED ENTITY NAME], [REGISTERED ADDRESS], Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. Questions, or a request to delete something, go to hello@chakh.in.

Two kinds of people use chakh

Restaurants, who buy the product, and diners, who tap a card at a table. We collect very different things from each.

If you are a diner

  • The preferences you set at the table — vegetarian or not, ingredients you avoid, spice level, language. These stay in your own browser. They are not sent to us with your name attached, because we never ask for your name.
  • Questions you type about a dish. The question and the dish's details go to Anthropic, which runs the model that answers you. Your identity is not sent. Please don't type personal or medical information into it.
  • Counts. Which dishes get opened and asked about, as totals. The restaurant sees that Kori Gassi was asked about forty times. It cannot see who asked.

We do not ask for your name, phone number or email. We do not build a profile of you. We do not sell anything to advertisers.

If you are a restaurant

  • The name, email and phone you send through the booking form, used to contact you about a demo and nothing else.
  • Your menu, dish details, ingredients and allergen declarations.
  • Billing details, handled by our payment provider. Card numbers never touch our servers.

Cookies

The site itself sets no cookies. Your consent choice is stored in your browser's local storage so we stop asking. Analytics, which does set a cookie, loads only if you press Accept — and pressing Decline is exactly as easy, which is the point. Change your mind by clearing site data for chakh.in.

Who else gets the data

  • Anthropic — runs the model that writes dish explanations.
  • Vercel — hosting.
  • Resend — delivers the booking-form email to us.
  • Google Analytics — only after you accept, with IP anonymisation on.

Nobody else. We do not sell data.

How long we keep it

Demo enquiries, two years or until you ask us to delete them. Diner preferences, in your browser only, until you clear it. Aggregate dish counts, indefinitely, because they contain nothing about any person.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to erase it, and withdraw consent. Mail hello@chakh.in and we will respond within 30 days. If we have not sorted it out, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

chakh is sold to restaurants and used by adults ordering food. We do not knowingly collect anything from anyone under 18.

Security

Everything runs over HTTPS. API keys are held server-side in environment variables, never in the browser. Access to restaurant data is limited to the people who need it to do the work.

Changes

If this changes materially we will email restaurant customers and change the date at the top.
