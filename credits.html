What this means if you change anything

CC BY-SA allows commercial use, including a paid product, as long as the credit stays visible. If you crop or edit one of these photographs, the edited version must also be released under the same licence. If you would rather avoid that, replace them with your own photographs, which is what a real restaurant customer would do anyway.

About the restaurant

Karavalli is a real restaurant at Vivanta Bengaluru, Residency Road, open since 20 June 1990. It is used here as an illustrative example and is not affiliated with chakh and has not endorsed it. Dish names and prices are drawn from its published menu. Its Google rating and review count were checked in September 2026 and will drift.

The guest reviews shown in the demo were written for the demo and are not real Google reviews.
